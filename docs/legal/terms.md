## 1. Using Toran

- Toran lists Hindu mandirs in the UK, their events and festival dates.
  Browsing is free and needs no account.
- We try hard to keep listings right, but **always check with the mandir
  before travelling**, especially for times and festival dates. Festival
  dates marked "Date not yet checked" are provisional.
- Don't misuse the site: no attempts to break in, overload it, copy the
  whole directory automatically, or use it to send spam.
- Buttons like "Directions", "Watch live", "Book tickets" and "Website"
  take you to other sites. Tickets, live streams and bookings are between
  you and the mandir or that site; Toran doesn't sell tickets or take
  payments.

## 2. Looking after a mandir's page

If you ask to look after a mandir's page, you confirm that:

- you're on that mandir's committee (or otherwise authorised by it) to
  manage its listing;
- what you add is accurate, lawful, and yours or the mandir's to share;
- you'll keep your login private and tell us if someone else may have
  used it.

We check every request with the mandir before approving it. We can remove
anyone's access if the mandir asks, or if we believe it has been misused.

**Content you add** (events, descriptions, links, photos, corrections)
stays the mandir's. You give us permission to publish it on Toran, in the
pictures shown when a page is shared, and later in our weekly email and
reminders, for as long as it's relevant. We may edit or remove anything
that's wrong, out of date, or breaks these terms.

**Photos.** A photo you add must show the mandir building only, with no
people in it, and must be yours or the mandir's to share (for example,
taken by someone on the committee). We'll remove a photo at once if it
shows a person, or if anyone with a good reason asks us to.

**Posters read automatically.** If you send a picture of a poster to be
read, check the events it finds before publishing them: you're
responsible for what goes on your page, as if you'd typed it.

## 3. Mandirs that don't want to be listed

Mandir names, towns and websites come from public sources. If a mandir
would rather not be listed, its committee can email us and we'll hide its
page **the same day**.
