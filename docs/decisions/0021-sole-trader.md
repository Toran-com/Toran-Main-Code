# 0021. Toran is run by Milan Patel as a sole trader

Date: 2026-11-12
Status: Accepted

## Context

Decision 0007 planned a private limited company, owned by both founders,
before any personal data was collected. In November 2026 Milan, who
builds and runs Toran day to day, chose to run it himself as a sole
trader instead: it costs nothing to set up, has one tax return a year
instead of company accounts, and suits a free service that earns little.
The other founder helps, but isn't developing the business. Milan has
registered with HMRC as self-employed and paid the ICO data protection
fee as a sole trader, trading as Toran.

## Decision

Toran is a trading name of **Milan Patel, sole trader**. He is the data
controller for everything the privacy notice describes, and the ICO
registration, the domain and the service accounts are in his name.
This replaces decision 0007.

## Consequences

- **Personal liability.** There is no company between Toran and Milan:
  claims, fines or debts are his personally. He has accepted this.
  Cyber and professional liability insurance is worth pricing once the
  pilot starts.
- **His name and an address are public.** The law requires the
  privacy notice, the terms and the site to say who runs Toran and give
  an address (the E-Commerce Regulations 2002 and the business names
  rules). The ICO register lists him as a sole trader trading as Toran.
- **The other founder is not a partner.** Two people running a business
  together without a company can be treated as a partnership, each
  liable for the other. A short note signed by both says Toran is Milan's
  business, what the other founder's role is, and that anything they made
  for Toran belongs to Milan.
- **Tax.** Toran's income and costs go on Milan's Self Assessment return
  each year (by 31 January), alongside any employment. Keep records and
  receipts. VAT only above the VAT threshold.
- **Accounts.** Use Toran's own email addresses (`hello@` and
  `privacy@toran.org.uk`) for its service accounts, with two-factor login
  on each (DPIA risk R10), so they're never tied up with personal mail.
- **Article 9(2)(d)** (not-for-profit religious bodies) still doesn't
  apply: Toran is a business, not the mandir.
- **When to look again.** Move to a limited company if Toran takes on
  staff, a partner, investment or real income, or once family accounts
  hold data about many thousands of people. Moving means a new ICO
  registration in the company's name, updating the privacy notice and
  terms, moving the accounts and an IP assignment from Milan to the
  company.
