# 0006. Collect the least personal data possible

Date: 2026-10-07
Status: Proposed

## Context

Where someone worships or volunteers reveals their religion, which is
special category data under UK GDPR. Some volunteers will be under 18.

## Decision

Browsing needs no account. The digest needs only an email and postcode.
Rota signups need a name and one contact detail. We do not record which
mandir someone belongs to. Every table with personal data has a retention
period. Register with the ICO and complete a DPIA before collecting any
personal data.

## Consequences

Fewer personalisation features early on. Much lower legal risk and an easy
promise to make to committees: we hold almost nothing about your devotees.
