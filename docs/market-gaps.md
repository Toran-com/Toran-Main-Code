# Market gaps — October 2026

What already exists for UK Hindus, checked against the original build brief
(`docs/source/build-brief-2026-10-02.pdf`). The brief assumed the Hindu-specific
space was "close to empty". It is not empty everywhere, and where it is filled
changes what this product should do first.

## What exists

| Need                                            | Who serves it today                                                                                                                                                                               | Gap?                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Panchang / festival dates for UK cities         | Drik Panchang (any city, Amanta/Purnimanta, Vaishnava Ekadashi, offline app); Bhakti Bharat has per-city pages for Leeds, Luton, Derby, Sandwell etc.; many panchang apps with vrat notifications | **No.** Saturated, free, trusted. A new engine is table stakes, not a moat.                                                           |
| Directory of UK mandirs                         | hindu-temples.uk — map and directory of 154 mandirs with traditions, opening hours, directions                                                                                                    | **No.** A static directory already exists.                                                                                            |
| "What's on at mandirs near me"                  | Hindu Events UK (hinduevents.info) — 33 events across 18 temples, London-centred, May–Dec 2026, events added by phoning or emailing one person; individual mandir websites and Facebook/WhatsApp  | **Yes — coverage and freshness.** Demand is proven (someone is doing it by hand), but nobody has current, broad, searchable coverage. |
| Festival → "which mandir near me is marking it" | Nobody joins the two. Calendars stop at the date; event lists don't know the calendar.                                                                                                            | **Yes.**                                                                                                                              |
| Mandir volunteer rotas                          | WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets. Generic tools (ChurchSuite) or India-built temple ERPs (e.g. Shlok ERP — darshan passes, pooja bookings)                                                        | **Yes.** Nothing UK-mandir-shaped and light enough for an unpaid committee.                                                           |
| Verified volunteering record for young people   | Nothing Hindu-specific in the UK. DofE has its own assessor flow; UCAS has no evidence upload.                                                                                                    | **Partial.** Real but narrower than the brief claims — see the critique.                                                              |

## What this means

1. **Do not lead with the panchang.** It is the most crowded part of the space.
   Keep festival dates as a supporting layer that links to events, and validate
   them against Drik Panchang rather than competing with it.
2. **Lead with current, local events.** It is the one consumer gap with proven
   demand and no adequate answer.
3. **Give mandirs a working seva rota.** It is the gap on the supply side, the
   reason a committee claims its listing, and the most likely future revenue.
4. **Treat Hindu Events UK as a possible partner, not only a competitor.** It has
   the relationships and a manual pipeline; this product has collection and
   tooling.
